Fuelaris Capital · Privacy Policy

Risk disclaimer: Investments in private and growth-stage IT companies involve market, liquidity, and execution risks; historical performance does not guarantee future results.

Privacy Policy

Introduction: Fuelaris Capital safeguards investor information used for allocation, governance, and execution communication.

Data collection: We collect identity, professional contact data, suitability information, and communication records submitted through forms and meetings.

Legal basis: Processing relies on legitimate interest, contractual preparation, and legal obligations for compliance.

Cookies: Essential cookies support security and session continuity; analytics cookies are used in aggregate form.

Retention: Records are retained according to regulatory retention schedules and then securely removed.

Third-party sharing: Data may be shared with custodians, legal advisors, auditors, and regulated service providers under confidentiality obligations.

Security: Encryption, access controls, logging, and periodic reviews protect confidentiality and integrity.

User rights: Investors can request access, correction, portability, restriction, or deletion where legally permitted.

International transfers: Cross-border transfers use contractual safeguards and equivalent protection standards.

Industry risk disclosure: Investment communications can include forward-looking assumptions exposed to market and liquidity shocks.
